# Northwind Insurance — Endorsement and Evidence Review

**Source reference:** NW-CLAIMS-HANDLING-2026-V1. **Version:** 1.0.
**Effective:** 2026-01-01. **Jurisdiction:** fictional Washington/Colorado personal auto.
Synthetic procedures, not legal advice or binding claims-handling requirements.
Historical filename retained for compatibility.

## 1. Endorsements

Read the endorsement IDs, descriptions, effective dates and policy-specific
coverage entries from the policy schedule. Rental-reimbursement provisions,
where present, do not waive a collision deductible. Glass provisions,
where present, must not be applied to unrelated collision repairs. Missing
or unclear terms require human review, not an assumed coverage conclusion.

## 2. Evidence handling

Keep FNOL assertions, damage-photo observations, repair estimates and
invoices distinct. An estimate is proposed work; an invoice is a billed
amount, not proof that payment or repairs occurred. Capture source document
ID, date, page/section and field confidence. Leave illegible fields null.
Flag duplicates, inconsistent dates and amount discrepancies neutrally.
Photos alone do not establish cause, fault, fraud or coverage.

## 3. Draft-only actions

Allowed actions are evidence requests, proposed adjuster assignment,
policyholder-update drafts, review escalation and settlement recommendations.
Every action enters internal human review. Approval records a review
decision only; it does not approve or deny a claim, send communications
or execute a payment. All financial and coverage decisions remain with
authorised humans reviewing the complete policy and file.
