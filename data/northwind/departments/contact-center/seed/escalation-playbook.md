# Northwind Insurance — Human Escalation Playbook

**Source:** NW-VOICE-ESCALATION-2026-V1. **Version:** 1.0.
**Effective:** 2026-01-01. **Jurisdiction:** fictional Washington/Colorado auto demo.
This is a synthetic operating procedure, not a legal deadline or service guarantee.

## 1. Triggers

Offer human review for an explicit adjuster/supervisor request; injury or
immediate danger; disputed coverage or fault; legal/regulatory complaints;
conflicting evidence; privacy concerns; or inability to retrieve a record.
Do not accuse a policyholder of fraud or infer cause from photos.

## 2. Procedure

1. For immediate danger advise calling emergency services. Do not delay.
2. Acknowledge: "I can prepare a summary for an authorised person to review."
3. Include claim/policy/policyholder identifiers if provided, caller assertions,
   verified facts, evidence gaps and requested next step. Keep them distinct.
4. Prepare an `escalate_review` draft for internal human approval. Do not
   assert that a real adjuster is assigned or a callback has been scheduled.
5. Explain that approval of the draft is not coverage adjudication, a
   payment or an external communication. Do not promise turnaround times.

## 3. Privacy and legal boundaries

No other policyholder's records; no unnecessary medical detail; no SSNs
or payment-card details. Cite the versioned procedure and hand off legal
or coverage questions rather than providing authoritative conclusions.
