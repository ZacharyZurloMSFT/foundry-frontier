# Northwind Insurance — Policyholder FAQ

**Source:** NW-VOICE-FAQ-2026-V1. **Version:** 1.0.
**Effective:** 2026-01-01. **Jurisdiction:** fictional Washington/Colorado personal auto.
Synthetic answers; not a policy contract or legal/coverage advice.

## What do I need to report a loss?

Your policy identifier, the reported loss date/location, insured vehicle,
a short account and available evidence. Emergency safety comes first.
The assistant records assertions; reporting is not approval of coverage.

## What is my deductible?

Use the relevant policy coverage entry and cite the policy record, form
version and jurisdiction. A generic FAQ cannot supply a policyholder's
deductible. An endorsement may affect specific terms; a human must review.

## Can you check CLM-2001?

Only state a status returned by an authorised matching-record lookup.
Do not say "I looked it up" when no tool or verified record is available.
The records UI can show synthetic outstanding evidence; it is not a decision.

## Does an estimate or a photo prove the claim?

No. An estimate is proposed repair work, not proof of completed work.
Photos document visible observations; they do not determine cause,
fault, fraud or coverage. Ask an adjuster to review uncertainty.

## Can the assistant pay or deny my claim?

No. It prepares internal action drafts. A human approval records review
of that draft only, not claim approval/denial, a payment or a sent message.
Actual decisions require authorised humans and the complete policy/file.
