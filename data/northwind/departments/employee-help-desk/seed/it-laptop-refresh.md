# Laptop Refresh Cadence

**Doc ID:** IT-420

Northwind refreshes issued laptops on a **3-year cadence** measured from
the ship date printed on your device asset tag. You will receive an
email 60 days before your device is due for refresh.

## Standard configurations

| Role type              | Default device                | Notes                             |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Corporate — general    | Contoso Book Pro 14 (i7/32GB) | Windows 11 Enterprise             |
| Corporate — engineer   | Contoso Book Pro 16 (i9/64GB) | Windows 11 Enterprise or Ubuntu LTS |
| Field adjuster          | Rugged Tablet 12"             | Windows 11 Enterprise; LTE modem  |

If your role has changed since your last refresh, note the new role in
the refresh ticket and IT will right-size the config.

## Data transfer

You have **10 business days** after receiving the new laptop to migrate
your data. Northwind uses **OneDrive Known Folder Move** for Documents,
Desktop, and Pictures — those transfer automatically as long as you're
signed in.

Return the old device via the pre-paid FedEx label included in the box.
Failure to return within 30 days results in a USD 850 payroll deduction.

## Off-cycle replacements

Damaged or lost devices are replaced in 2–3 business days. Open a
`Device Replacement` ticket and select the reason. Repeated loss (3+
within 24 months) triggers a manager review.
