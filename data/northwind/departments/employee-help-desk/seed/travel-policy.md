# Travel Policy

**Doc ID:** FIN-320

## Booking tool

All business travel must be booked through **Concur**. Bookings made
outside Concur are only reimbursed with prior written VP approval.

## Air travel

- **≤ 4 hours flight time:** economy.
- **4–8 hours flight time:** economy; premium-economy allowed for
  Director-level and above.
- **> 8 hours flight time:** business class allowed for Director-level
  and above; economy or premium-economy otherwise.

Northwind does not book first-class travel under any circumstance.

## Lodging

Concur enforces per-city hotel caps (e.g., NYC USD 350/night, Toronto
CAD 275/night). Overrides require pre-trip VP approval.

## Ground transportation

See `expense-policy.md` for per-trip rules on rideshare, rental cars,
and mileage.

## International travel

Requires VP approval and a Trip Risk assessment through the corporate
security portal. High-risk destinations require sign-off from the CSO.

## Combining personal travel

You may combine business travel with personal segments (e.g., extended
weekend) provided:

- The itinerary shown to Concur is business-only.
- Personal segments are self-booked with personal payment.
- Any incremental cost (e.g., changing the return flight) is borne by
  the employee.
