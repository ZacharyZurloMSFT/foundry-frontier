# Parental Leave Policy

**Doc ID:** HR-025

Northwind Insurance offers **16 weeks of fully-paid parental leave**
for all eligible full-time employees, regardless of gender or path to
parenthood.

## Eligibility

You must have completed 90 days of continuous employment before the
event (birth, adoption, or legal guardianship placement) for full
eligibility. Employees with 30–89 days of tenure receive 8 weeks paid.

## Usage rules

- Must be taken within **12 months** of the event.
- Can be taken in up to **2 blocks** — for example, 12 weeks immediately
  after the event and 4 weeks later in the year.
- Fully-paid means base salary + on-target bonus prorate; commissions
  and overtime are excluded.

## Interaction with short-term disability

For birthing parents, Northwind's parental leave runs **in addition to**
the standard 6-week (vaginal) or 8-week (cesarean) short-term disability
benefit — so a birthing parent typically receives 22–24 weeks of paid
leave.

## Return-to-work support

Lactation rooms are provided at every corporate office. Field
adjusters returning from leave receive a graduated case load for the
first 4 weeks (75% capacity).
