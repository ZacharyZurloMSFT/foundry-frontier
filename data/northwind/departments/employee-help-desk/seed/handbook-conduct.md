# Northwind Insurance — Code of Conduct

**Effective:** January 1, 2026 · **Owner:** People & Culture · **Doc ID:** HR-001

## Our commitments

- **Safety first.** Every Northwind employee — from field adjusters at
  a loss scene to a controller in the Toronto HQ — is empowered to
  stop work if a task cannot be performed safely.
- **Customer respect.** We speak plainly, we do what we say, and we
  never disparage a customer's site, staff, or equipment.
- **Data stewardship.** Policyholder records, adjuster notes, and insurance
  claims are confidential. Do not export them to personal devices or
  external email.
- **Ethical vendor conduct.** Gifts over USD 75 from a supplier must be
  declined or logged with your manager within 5 business days.

## Reporting concerns

Contact your manager first. If that isn't appropriate, use the
confidential ethics line at `ethics@northwind.example` or the anonymous
hotline number posted in every break room. Northwind does not tolerate
retaliation against good-faith reports.

## Enforcement

Violations are reviewed by People & Culture in partnership with the
employee's business unit. Consequences range from coaching to termination
depending on severity and history.
