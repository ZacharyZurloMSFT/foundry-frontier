# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Doc ID:** HR-014 · **Applies to:** All non-field-adjuster employees

## Schedule

Northwind Insurance operates on a **3-2 hybrid schedule** for corporate
roles: three days in the assigned office, two days remote. The three
in-office days must include **Tuesday and Wednesday** so teams can
overlap for planning. The third day is chosen by your team lead.

Field adjusters are exempt from this policy — they follow the
field-review schedule set by their regional operations manager.

## Home-office stipend

Each corporate employee receives a **USD 750 one-time home-office
stipend** on hire and a **USD 300 annual refresh** thereafter. Eligible
expenses: monitor, chair, desk, headset, noise-cancelling headphones,
webcam. Not eligible: personal furniture, tablets, gaming peripherals.

Submit receipts through Expensify with the category
`Home Office Stipend`. Reimbursement lands on the next pay cycle.

## Full-remote exceptions

Full-remote arrangements are approved by the SVP of the business unit
and require a signed remote-work addendum. Full-remote employees are
not eligible for the office-day-adjacent stipend increases.
