# Security Training & Phishing Simulation

**Doc ID:** SEC-501

## Annual required training

Every employee must complete **Northwind Security Fundamentals** within
30 days of hire and re-certify annually. The course runs about 40
minutes and lives at `learning.northwind.com/security-2026`.

Non-completion by the 30-day mark triggers:
- Day 30: reminder email + manager cc
- Day 45: manager escalation
- Day 60: temporary revocation of ZTA VPN access until completion

## Phishing simulations

Northwind sends **8 simulated phishing emails per employee per year** —
about 2 per quarter. Simulations are branded with the Northwind
Security logo in the footer so the sender is identifiable *after* click.

- **First click in 12 months:** a 5-minute microtraining pops up.
- **Second click in 12 months:** manager notified + 20-minute retraining.
- **Third click in 12 months:** SOC review + role-based access audit.

Reporting a real phish through the Report Phish button never counts as
a click — please use it liberally.

## Role-specific modules

- Field techs: **customer-site data handling** (annual, 15 min).
- Engineers with prod access: **secure coding** (annual, 60 min).
- Anyone handling PII: **privacy fundamentals** (annual, 30 min).
