# Medical Benefits

**Doc ID:** BEN-101 · **Provider:** Contoso Health (US) / Alberta Blue (CA)

## Plan tiers

| Plan          | Employee cost / month | Family cost / month | Deductible (individual / family) |
| ------------- | --------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Core**      | USD 40                | USD 220             | 1,500 / 3,000                    |
| **Plus**      | USD 95                | USD 380             | 750 / 1,500                      |
| **Premium**   | USD 175               | USD 620             | 250 / 500                        |

All three tiers include **preventive care at 100%** with no deductible.

## HSA eligibility

Only the **Core** plan is HSA-eligible. Northwind contributes USD 1,000
per year for individual coverage and USD 2,000 for family coverage,
deposited in equal quarterly amounts.

## Dependent coverage

Spouses, domestic partners, and children up to age 26 are eligible.
Documentation requirements are listed in `dependent-verification.md`.

## Enrollment windows

- **New hire:** first 30 days from start date.
- **Open enrollment:** October 15 – November 15 annually, effective
  January 1.
- **Qualifying life events:** marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other
  coverage — submit within 30 days of the event.

Late elections require carrier appeal and are not guaranteed.
