# PTO (Paid Time Off) Policy

**Doc ID:** HR-021

## Accrual

- **Years 0–2:** 15 business days per calendar year, accruing at 1.25
  days per month.
- **Years 3–5:** 20 business days per year, accruing at 1.67 days per
  month.
- **Years 6+:** 25 business days per year, accruing at 2.08 days per
  month.

Accrual starts on your first day of employment. There is no probation
delay.

## Carryover

You may carry **up to 5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year.
Unused days above the cap expire on January 31.

## Blackout dates

Claims Concierge review teams observe blackout periods during
**Q4 peak (Nov 15 – Dec 24)** and **spring startup (Mar 15 – Apr 15)**.
PTO during these windows must be requested at least 45 days in advance
and requires regional-manager approval.

Corporate roles have no blackout dates.

## Requesting PTO

Submit through Workday. Manager approval is required for any request of
2+ consecutive days. Same-day sick time does not count against PTO —
see `sick-leave.md` (referenced but not attached in this snapshot).
