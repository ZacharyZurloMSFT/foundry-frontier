# Expense Policy

**Doc ID:** FIN-301 · **Owner:** Corporate Finance

## Per-diems (US domestic travel)

| Category    | Per diem  | Notes                          |
| ----------- | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| Breakfast   | USD 18    | Waived if hotel-provided       |
| Lunch       | USD 25    | Includes non-alcoholic drink   |
| Dinner      | USD 55    | Includes non-alcoholic drink   |
| Incidentals | USD 10    | Tips, parking meters, laundry  |

Actual receipts are required for any single meal over USD 40.

## Ground transportation

- Uber / Lyft **Regular / X** class only. UberBLACK, Comfort Plus, and
  Wait & Save premium are not reimbursable without pre-approval.
- Rental cars: economy or intermediate class for solo travel.
- Personal vehicle mileage: IRS standard rate (2026: USD 0.71 / mile).

## Receipts

All expenses ≥ USD 25 require a receipt uploaded to Expensify.
Missing-receipt affidavits are permitted up to twice per calendar year.

## Approval routing

- ≤ USD 500: direct manager
- USD 501 – 2,500: manager + VP
- USD 2,501+: VP + Finance controller

Report submission deadline: **30 days** after trip completion or
purchase date. Reports older than 90 days require CFO approval.
