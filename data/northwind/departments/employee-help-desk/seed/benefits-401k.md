# 401(k) / RRSP Retirement Plan

**Doc ID:** BEN-201 · **Provider:** Fabrikam Retirement (US) / RBC Direct (CA)

## Match

Northwind matches **100% of the first 4%** of eligible pay and **50% of
the next 2%** — so a full contribution of 6% earns a 5% match.

## Vesting

- **Employee contributions:** always 100% vested.
- **Employer match:** graded vesting over 3 years —
  - Year 1: 33%
  - Year 2: 66%
  - Year 3: 100%

Vesting resets on rehire only if the break in service exceeds 5 years.

## Eligibility

All employees are enrolled automatically at **3% contribution** starting
on day 60. You may change the amount or opt out at any time.

## Contribution limits

For 2026: USD 23,000 employee contribution limit; USD 30,500 catch-up
limit for employees age 50+. Northwind does not permit after-tax
contributions above the IRS limit.

## Rollover and loans

Rollovers from prior employer plans are supported. Loans are permitted
up to 50% of vested balance (max USD 50,000) with a 5-year repayment
term via payroll deduction.
