# Learning & Development

**Doc ID:** BEN-401

Northwind Insurance reimburses **up to USD 2,500 per employee per
calendar year** for approved learning and development spend.

## Eligible

- Technical certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, HVAC/R
  journeyman, PE licensure, etc.)
- Online courses from accredited providers (Coursera, edX, Pluralsight,
  Udemy Business, MasterClass Business).
- Books directly related to your role — receipts required, capped at
  USD 300 per year.
- Conference registration + reasonable travel (see `travel-policy.md`).
- Professional-membership dues (max 2 memberships per year).

## Not eligible

- General-education degree tuition — see the separate
  `tuition-reimbursement.md` (managed by People & Culture).
- Coaching or executive-coaching services.
- Purely-recreational courses.

## How to submit

1. Get manager pre-approval via Workday › Learning.
2. Complete the course / obtain the credential.
3. Submit receipts to Expensify with category `L&D Reimbursement` and
   attach proof of completion.

Reimbursement lands on the next pay cycle after Finance approval.
