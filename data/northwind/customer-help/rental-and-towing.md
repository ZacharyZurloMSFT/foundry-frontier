# Fictional Northwind help: Rental cars and towing

## An illustration of rental reimbursement

A policy can include a rental reimbursement endorsement, and the policy page lists the endorsements on the policy.

In a fictional illustration, rental reimbursement may be considered up to $30 per day for 30 days, with a maximum of $900.

The illustrative endorsement says that rental reimbursement may be considered only after a qualified human verifies a covered loss, the need for a rental and the receipts.

The $30, 30 day and $900 figures are illustrations only, and the endorsement on the policy page of a policyholder is the authority.

## What the portal does not do

- The portal does not authorize a rental car.
- The portal does not book a rental car or dispatch a tow truck.
- The portal does not contact rental companies, tow operators or repair shops.
- Any rental reimbursement may be considered only by a qualified human during claim review.

## What to do yourself

- In an emergency, contact local emergency services, because the portal is not an emergency service.
- If a vehicle needs towing, contact a roadside or towing service yourself, because this demonstration cannot send one.
- Keep every receipt for a rental car or a tow, because a human reviewer may ask for receipts.
- If a case asks for receipts, attach them as PDF, JPEG or PNG documents that are no larger than 10 MiB.
- Use synthetic receipts only, because this demonstration is not for real expenses.

## Where to look

The endorsements list on a policy page shows whether a rental reimbursement endorsement is present on the policy.

The wording section of a policy page shows the illustrative wording for each endorsement.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about rental cars and towing is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
