# Fictional Northwind help: What happens after you report a loss

## The short answer

After a policyholder submits a loss report, the portal opens a case with a claim number, and a human adjuster manages that case.

A case moves through five statuses, and the portal shows the current status on My claims and on the case page.

Nothing is approved, denied, settled or paid automatically, because a person decides every coverage question.

## The five statuses

- The reported status means the portal has received the loss report and a person will review the case.
- The evidence requested status means a person has asked for evidence, such as an incident statement, damage photographs or a repair estimate.
- The evidence received status means evidence has been recorded on the case so that a person can read it.
- The adjuster review status means the case is with a human adjuster for review.
- The customer update prepared status only means that a person is preparing an update inside the portal, and it is not a decision about coverage or payment.

## Who acts at each step

- The policyholder submits the loss report and attaches the evidence that a case requests.
- A human adjuster manages every case, which includes requesting evidence, reading documents and preparing updates.
- Only a person changes the status of a case, and a person may return a case to the evidence requested status when more evidence is needed.
- The portal itself does not decide coverage, settle a claim or make a payment.

## Customer-visible updates

A customer-visible update is a short message that a person writes on a case and chooses to show to the policyholder.

Customer-visible updates appear in the portal under Case updates and in the case timeline.

The portal sends no external emails, text messages or phone calls, so a policyholder reads updates by opening My claims.

Internal notes written by staff are never shown to policyholders.

## While you wait

- Open My claims to see the current status and the outstanding evidence for each case.
- Keep the claim number, because the claim number identifies the case.
- Attach requested evidence to the case when the documents section is available.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about the claim steps is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
