# Fictional Northwind help: Billing, premiums and claim payments

## What this demonstration does not do

- The demonstration takes no premium payments.
- The demonstration issues no claim payments.
- The demonstration offers no quotes and no policy purchases.
- The portal has no payment page, and no real money moves in this demonstration.

## Premiums

A premium is the price that an insurer charges for a policy.

The policy pages in this demonstration show no premium amounts and no due dates, because the portal has no billing system.

The demonstration collects no premium, so a policyholder never pays a premium in the portal.

## Claim payments

A claim payment is money that an insurer may send to a policyholder after a person decides the outcome of a claim.

This demonstration never sends a claim payment, and the portal has no way to execute a payment.

## Never enter real payment details

Never enter a real card number, bank account number or other real payment detail anywhere in the portal or in an assistant chat.

Loss reports, evidence documents and assistant questions are for synthetic information only.

## Settlement recommendations

A settlement recommendation is an internal draft that stays pending review by a person.

A settlement recommendation is not a settlement offer, and a settlement recommendation cannot execute a payment.

Internal drafts and internal notes are not shown to policyholders.

## Questions about money

The coverage schedule shows limits and deductibles, and a schedule figure is not a promise of an amount.

The portal does not predict settlement amounts or payment timing.

A person reviews every claim, and the portal never decides coverage or the outcome of a claim.

## Where to look

- Deductibles and limits are on the policy page under the coverage schedule.
- Claim progress is on My claims.
- The meaning of insurance terms is in the glossary help article.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about billing and payments is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
