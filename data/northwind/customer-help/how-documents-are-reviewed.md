# Fictional Northwind help: How your documents are read and reviewed

## Automatic reading is optional

The portal can read documents automatically, which is also called extraction, but the operator may switch automatic reading off.

When automatic reading is switched off, the portal still stores documents and an adjuster can still review them by hand.

Automatic reading never decides anything about a claim.

## Suggestions are untrusted

Anything that automatic reading produces is only an untrusted suggestion.

A suggestion can be wrong, incomplete or missing, so the portal labels each suggestion as untrusted and says that human review is required.

A confidence figure is extra information only, and a confidence figure does not confirm a fact.

## An adjuster confirms each field

- A human adjuster compares each suggested field with the original document.
- The adjuster makes a separate decision for each field, which can be to accept the suggestion, to enter a corrected value or to clear the field.
- The adjuster must confirm that the original source was inspected before a review is saved.
- The portal saves the reviewed value separately from the original suggestion.

The reviewed fields can include the claim number, the policy number, the policyholder number, the loss date, an estimate total and an invoice total.

Confirming a field does not approve coverage, settle a claim or make a payment.

## What policyholders can see

Policyholders can see the documents that are attached to their own claims.

Policyholders see only human-reviewed facts and customer-visible case updates.

Internal notes are never shown to policyholders.

If an adjuster has not confirmed any field yet, the case shows that no fields have been confirmed.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about document review is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
