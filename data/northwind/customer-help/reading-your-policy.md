# Fictional Northwind help: Reading your schedule, wording and endorsements

## What a policy page shows

A policy page shows a coverage schedule that lists each coverage with its limit and its deductible.

A policy page lists the endorsements on the policy, and each endorsement shows a version, a state and effective dates.

A policy page also offers the policy wording and its source references under Read policy wording and source references.

## Versions, effective dates and jurisdiction

- Each wording has a version number, a state and effective dates.
- The effective dates show the period when that version of the wording applies.
- The jurisdiction of a policy is the US state that is shown on the policy page.
- The policy wording in this demonstration is illustrative wording only.

## Citations

The versioned citations section lists wording sections, and each citation shows a reference, a version, effective dates and a source.

When the Claims Concierge is enabled, the Claims Concierge cites these references, so a policyholder can find the cited text on the policy page.

## Review flags

A review flag is a note that the portal records for a human reviewer when a detail of a loss report differs from the policy record.

- The loss outside policy period flag means the reported loss date is outside the effective dates of the policy.
- The loss jurisdiction mismatch flag means the loss state differs from the state of the policy.
- The loss outside wording period flag means the loss date is outside the effective dates of the wording version on file.
- The loss outside endorsement period flag means the loss date is outside the effective dates of an endorsement.

A review flag is not a denial and does not decide coverage.

A human adjuster considers each review flag together with the evidence and the policy wording.

The policy page of a policyholder is the authority for that policy, and the help articles give general information only.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about policy pages is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
