# Fictional Northwind help: What evidence to send and in what format

## What a case may ask for

A policyholder should send the evidence that a case lists under Next evidence steps on the case page.

A person decides which evidence a case needs, and the typical requests are an incident statement, damage photographs and a repair estimate.

- An incident statement is a short written account of what happened, including the date and the place of the loss.
- Damage photographs are clear pictures that show the damage to the insured vehicle from more than one angle.
- A repair estimate is a document from a repair shop that lists the repairs and their expected cost.

Missing evidence leads to a request or a human review, and missing evidence never causes an automatic decision.

## Formats and limits

- An evidence document must be a PDF, JPEG or PNG file.
- Each evidence document can be at most 10 MiB, and an empty file is not accepted.
- A claim can hold at most four documents.
- Only synthetic documents belong in this demonstration, so never attach real personal records, real photographs or real financial documents.

## How to attach evidence to a claim

1. Open My claims and open the case that needs evidence.
2. Find the Documents and evidence review section on the case page.
3. Choose a source document, which is an allowlisted synthetic sample or, when the operator allows uploads, a synthetic file from your device.
4. Tick the checkbox that confirms the document contains synthetic information only.
5. Select the button that stores the document.

The attach form appears only when evidence handling is enabled for the demonstration.

## After a document is attached

The portal records the document on the case, and the case timeline shows that evidence was registered for human review.

Attaching a document does not change the status of the case, because only a person changes the status.

If the connection fails while attaching a document, read the current documents on the case before retrying the same document.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about evidence is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
