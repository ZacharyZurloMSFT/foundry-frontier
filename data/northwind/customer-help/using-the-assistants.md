# Fictional Northwind help: Using the Claims Concierge and Help Center

## Two assistants

The Claims Concierge answers questions about the signed-in policyholder's own policies and claims.

The Help Center answers general how-to questions by selecting exact excerpts from the help articles.

The assistants are available only when the operator has enabled them for the demonstration.

## What the assistants will do

- The Claims Concierge explains claim statuses, outstanding evidence, review flags and the wording of the policyholder's own policy, and it cites the wording that it uses.
- The Help Center shows exact excerpts from the help articles together with a citation to the article that each excerpt came from.
- Both assistants check an answer before showing it, and an answer that sounds like a coverage, claim or payment decision is withheld.
- When the help articles do not answer a question, the Help Center says so instead of guessing.

## What the assistants will not do

- Neither assistant can change, submit, cancel or send anything.
- Neither assistant decides coverage, states a settlement amount or promises payment timing.
- The Help Center does not read personal records, so questions about a policyholder's own policy or claim belong to the Claims Concierge.
- Neither assistant gives legal, medical, tax or financial advice.

## Tips for good answers

- Ask one clear question at a time.
- Say what you want to know, such as what happens after a loss report is submitted.
- Do not enter real personal data in an assistant question.
- Select Stop to end an answer at any time, or start a new conversation when you want a fresh start.

## A person decides

A human adjuster reviews every claim, and an assistant answer is never a coverage decision.

Assistant chats are not stored in the browser, and chats expire.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about the assistants is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
