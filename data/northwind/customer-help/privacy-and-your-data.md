# Fictional Northwind help: Privacy and what staff can see

## Synthetic data only

Every record in this demonstration is synthetic, and no real person, vehicle, loss or payment detail belongs in the portal.

Policyholders confirm that each loss report and each evidence document contains synthetic information only.

## What policyholders can see

- Policyholders see only their own policies and claims.
- Policyholders see human-reviewed facts and customer-visible case updates.
- Internal notes are never shown to policyholders.
- The assigned adjuster of a case is not shown to policyholders.

## What staff can see

Staff accounts have roles that the server assigns, and a role decides which cases an account can open.

Staff who review claims can see the case record, the documents and the internal notes, because staff need them to do the review.

## How access is decided

Policyholders sign in with Microsoft Entra, and the server assigns the role of each account.

A role cannot be selected in the portal, so a policyholder cannot switch to a staff view.

A policyholder cannot open the records of another policyholder.

## Assistants and your records

- The Claims Concierge reads only the records of the signed-in policyholder.
- The Help Center reads only the general help articles and reads no personal records.
- Neither assistant can change a record.

## Chats, sign-in and the browser

- Assistant chats are not stored in the browser.
- Assistant chats expire after a limited time.
- Sign-in tokens stay in memory and are never stored in browser storage.
- Signing out of the portal clears the session.

## If you are unsure

Do not enter real personal details, real loss information, payment details or sensitive documents anywhere in the portal.

A person reviews every claim, and the portal never decides coverage on its own.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about privacy is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
