# Fictional Northwind help: Getting a person, escalation and complaints

## A person reviews every claim

A human adjuster manages every claim, and the portal never decides coverage or claim outcomes on its own.

Assigned adjusters are not shown to policyholders in the portal, so a policyholder does not choose or contact an adjuster.

## How to ask for a review in the portal

- Submitting a loss report opens a case that a person reviews.
- Attaching requested evidence to a case registers the evidence for human review.
- A person reviews every case, so a policyholder does not need to send a separate request to get a human review.
- Opening My claims shows the current status, the outstanding evidence, the case updates and the case timeline of each case.

## What the demonstration does not do

- The demonstration sends no external emails, text messages or phone calls.
- The portal has no message channel to an adjuster, and the assistants cannot send a message to staff.

## Escalation

Staff may escalate a case to a supervisor during review.

A policyholder sees only the customer-visible updates that a person records on the case.

## Checking on a case

A policyholder can open My claims at any time to read the current status of a case.

The status shows where the case is, and the status does not show a decision.

A case may stay in one status while a person reviews evidence, so a policyholder should read the case updates for new information.

## Complaints

The demonstration has no complaint form and no real complaint process, because the insurer and every record are fictional.

## Accessibility

- The portal pages are designed to work with a keyboard.
- The portal pages are designed to work with a screen reader.
- Each page offers a skip to main content link, and status messages are announced to screen readers.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about contacting a person is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
