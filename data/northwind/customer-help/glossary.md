# Fictional Northwind help: Insurance terms in plain English

## Claims terms

- A claim is a case that a policyholder opens by reporting a loss under a policy, and a person reviews the case.
- First notice of loss is the first report of a loss, and the Report a loss form in the portal is where a policyholder gives first notice of loss.
- An adjuster is a person who reviews a claim, reads the evidence and manages the case.
- Outstanding evidence is evidence that a case has asked for and that has not been completed yet.
- A review flag is a note that the portal records for a human reviewer when a detail differs from the policy record, and a review flag is not a denial.

## Policy terms

- A deductible is the part of a loss that the policyholder bears before any insurance amount may be considered.
- A limit is the highest amount that a coverage may consider for a loss, as listed on the policy schedule.
- An endorsement is an addition to a policy that adds to or changes the policy wording, such as a rental reimbursement endorsement or a glass endorsement.
- Wording is the written text of the policy terms, and each wording has a version, a state and effective dates.
- The policy schedule is the part of a policy page that lists each coverage with its limit and its deductible.

## Date and place terms

- The loss date is the date when the loss happened, and the portal compares the loss date with the effective dates of the policy.
- Jurisdiction means the US state that applies to a policy or to a loss, such as WA or CO.

## Demonstration terms

- Synthetic means fictional and made up for a demonstration, so synthetic information has no real person or real loss behind it.
- A customer-visible update is a case update that a person has chosen to show to the policyholder.

Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this help article about insurance terms is a synthetic demonstration, not a coverage decision.
